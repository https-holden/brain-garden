# The kepano method

Source: https://stephango.com/vault and the posts it links to. `references/method.md` is a
rule-by-rule digest in our own words; read it before answering, and read the posts themselves
for his phrasing. `references/divergences.md` lists where this vault deliberately departs.

Vault mechanics (which folder, which template, which property) live in the vault's
`CLAUDE.md`. This skill is the *why* and the *practice*; `CLAUDE.md` is the *where*.

## The rules in one screen

His own list, in his order:

1. One vault. Don't split content into several.
2. Avoid folders for organisation. Folders say what a note *is*; `categories` says what it is *about*.
3. Avoid non-standard Markdown.
4. Always pluralise categories and tags.
5. Link profusely. Link the first mention of anything. Unresolved links are breadcrumbs, not errors.
6. `YYYY-MM-DD` dates everywhere.
7. Ratings are integers 1 to 7.
8. One to-do list per week, rewritten from scratch.

Plus the practices the post describes rather than lists:

- Almost every note starts from a template, so properties exist from the first note.
- Property names and values are reused across kinds; names are short; anything that could ever hold two values is a list.
- Daily notes exist to be linked to, not written in.
- Evergreen notes are single ideas titled as claims you could use in a sentence.
- Write your rules down in a style guide you can see. Consistency turns a process into a style.

## How to audit

1. Identify the kind or workflow in question; read its template, its Base and two or three real notes.
2. Walk the rules above and `references/method.md`. For each: follows, departs, or not applicable.
3. Before flagging a departure, check `references/divergences.md`. Listed departures are deliberate; never report them as fixes.
4. Report a short list, worst first. A finding names the rule, the evidence (file and line) and the smallest change that would satisfy it. Do not apply changes during an audit unless asked.
5. Tell three kinds of finding apart: a method rule broken; a vault convention (from `CLAUDE.md`) broken; a place where the vault has no convention yet and kepano offers one.

## Answering "what would kepano do"

Quote the rule, cite the reference, then translate it into this vault's terms (folder,
template, Base, property). If the vault already diverges on purpose, say so and give the kepano
version anyway so the owner can choose. When kepano is silent on something, say that plainly
and do not invent a rule.
