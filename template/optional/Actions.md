# Actions

The action log: one line for everything done on @@OWNER@@'s behalf, newest last, appended and
never edited. It is how you see later what was done, why, and who asked, without a team
channel to look back through. Format:

`- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM What was done, with a [[link]]. Why: the reason. Chose: any choice made and the options passed over. Asked by: who, and where.`

`Chose:` can be left out when there was nothing to choose. The lint warns about a line that
does not start with a date and time or is missing `Why:` or `Asked by:`.
